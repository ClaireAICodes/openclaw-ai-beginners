## Preliminary Insights
- Healthcare is the most pressing compliance frontier due to semantic attack vectors (prompt injection to extract PHI) vs traditional network attacks
- HIPAA deployment patterns: BAA with AI provider, self-hosted models for full isolation, PHI detection/redaction pre-processing
- GDPR and EU AI Act (Aug 2026 enforcement) creating urgency for enterprise governance frameworks
- Data residency is a key selling point: OpenClaw keeps data on-premises, unlike cloud-only SaaS agents
- IBC framework emerging as standard: track Identity (which agent), Boundaries (tool permissions), Context (what data it can access)
- Auditing is a gap: autonomous agent actions may bypass traditional logs; need dedicated agent audit trails
- Enterprise readiness: skills must be vetted, sandboxed, and systems with production data must be off-limits to agents
- Market opportunity: compliance-as-a-service for OpenClaw deployments (HIPAA/GDPR hardening)
