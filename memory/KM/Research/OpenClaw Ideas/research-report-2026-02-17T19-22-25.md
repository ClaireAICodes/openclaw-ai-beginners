## Preliminary Insights & Recommendations

### 1. Market Position & Opportunity

Competitors include n8n (visual workflow), custom scripts, and cloud AI agents. OpenClaw's differentiator is **autonomous reasoning + execution** without explicit flowchart design.

**Opportunity size:**
- Rapidly growing AI agent market (2026 is breakout year per multiple sources)
