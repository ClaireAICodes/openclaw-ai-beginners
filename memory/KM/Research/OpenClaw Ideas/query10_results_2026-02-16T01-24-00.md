## Preliminary Insights
- **Cost reduction spectrum**: 60-97% savings possible depending on baseline and optimization depth
- **Three-pronged approach**:
  1. Architectural: local models (Ollama, LM Studio) for zero marginal cost, fallback to cloud for complex tasks
  2. Configuration: simplify injection files (3,000-14,000 tokens overhead per call), move instructions to skills
  3. Operational: route heartbeats through local models, use Claude Pro subscription if >$20/month API bill
- **Quantified savings**: Real case studies show $347→$68/month, $2-5/day idle cost elimination, $0.38/session × 20/day = $7.60/day
- **Noise floor problem**: Base token cost per call is surprisingly high (3-14K tokens even for simple queries) — simplifying injection helps most
- **Token Optimizer skill**: Community-developed, integrated into custom forks, recommended for easy deployment
- **Model routing economics**: Use strongest model for reasoning tasks but route routine queries to cheaper models (Claude Haiku, GPT-4o mini, local Llama)
- **Enterprise scale**: At 20+ sessions daily, annual savings can reach thousands; token optimization becomes ROI-positive investment of time
- **Rate limit mitigation**: Smart routing prevents hitting tier limits (e.g., Anthropic 50K/min), enabling 24/7 operation without throttling
