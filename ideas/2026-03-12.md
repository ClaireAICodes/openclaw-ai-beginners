### [GREEN] Comprehensive Net Worth & Cash Flow Tracker
**Category:** Lifestyle & Habits / Passive Income & Businesses
**Risk Level:** green (read-only financial data aggregation, no transactions)
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
A unified financial dashboard that aggregates all assets and liabilities to provide a real-time net worth calculation, asset allocation breakdown, and progress toward financial independence. Connects to bank accounts (via read-only APIs or Plaid), crypto wallets (DeBank, Zapper), investment platforms (Interactive Brokers, Alpaca), and debt accounts. Includes cash flow forecasting, tax liability estimates, and anomaly alerts (unexpected withdrawals, fees).

**Rationale:**
Master seeks passive income streams and long-term wealth growth. Without a consolidated view, it's impossible to optimize allocation or measure true progress. This tracker turns fragmented financial data into actionable intelligence: see which assets are performing, identify excessive fees, track debt paydown, and project retirement timelines. It turns financial management from a chore into a strategic advantage, aligning with his preference for data-driven optimization.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Research and integrate secure read-only connectors: bank feeds (Plaid, Yodlee), crypto wallet aggregators, brokerage APIs, crypto exchanges
- Normalize data into common schema: accounts, balances, transactions, timestamps
- Calculate net worth, asset allocation (crypto, stocks, cash, retirement), debt-to-asset ratio
- Build cash flow predictor based on recurring income/expenses and investment returns
- Add tax estimation module (capital gains, income tax) with jurisdiction-specific rules
- Design alert system: large withdrawals, low balances, unusually high fees, missed dividend payments
- Create simple dashboard (web or terminal) with charts (net worth trend, allocation pie, cash flow)
- Ensure all data stored locally with optional encrypted backups; no external transmission beyond API fetches

### [GREEN] Social & Dating Activity Tracker + Insights Engine
**Category:** Lifestyle & Habits / Social & Dating
**Risk Level:** green (local notes only, no external communications)
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
A private journaling and analytics tool for tracking dating and social interactions: dates, conversations, matches on apps, follow-ups, and outcomes. Uses LLM to analyze patterns (what types of conversations lead to successful dates, which platforms yield best matches), provides gentle reminders for follow-ups, and generates weekly insights to improve social success. All data stays local; no cloud sync unless explicitly enabled.

**Rationale:**
Master is actively exploring dating and social connections but may benefit from systematic tracking to identify what works and what doesn't. Manual notes are scattered and rarely reviewed. A dedicated tracker with pattern analysis would help him refine his approach, remember important details about people, and optimize his time and energy. It respects privacy while providing data-driven social coaching—aligning with his preference for optimization in all life domains.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Build local database (SQLite) to store interactions: person, date/time, platform, conversation highlights, outcome, follow-up tasks
- Create quick-entry CLI/TUI for logging a date or match in under 30 seconds
- Add reminder system for follow-ups (3 days later, etc.) with optional Telegram reminders (YELLOW)
- Implement pattern analysis: correlate message length, emoji use, meeting location with success rates
- Weekly report: top conversation starters, best days/times, common rejection reasons, improvement suggestions
- Privacy-first design: all data encrypted at rest; optional encrypted backup to remote storage
- Integrate with existing OpenClaw memory to avoid logging sensitive info in public contexts
- Add "do not disturb" mode for periods when Master wants a break from tracking
