### [GREEN] Passive Audience Building Engine
**Category:** Web3 & Crypto / Passive Income & Businesses
**Risk Level:** green
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
An automated system that researches trending Web3/crypto narratives (using the exa-tool and crypto-reporter skills), generates LinkedIn thought-leadership posts and Twitter threads that position Master as a credible voice at the intersection of corporate training and Web3, and publishes them on a scheduled cadence. Builds audience and authority passively — the foundation for future monetization (courses, consulting, NFT drops, affiliate deals). Also auto-generates Paragraph blog posts from the same research feed, feeding the content pipeline with minimal additional effort.

**Rationale:**
Master is a corporate trainer exploring Web3 — that's a rare and valuable intersection. Most crypto influencers lack enterprise credibility; most trainers lack crypto knowledge. By consistently publishing at this intersection, Master builds a unique personal brand that compounds over time. The research infrastructure already exists (hourly crypto reports, exa-tool). This just adds a content synthesis + publishing layer. Audience = future customers for courses, consulting, and any Web3 products.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Build narrative synthesis module: pull from hourly crypto reports, extract top 3 trending stories with corporate training angle (e.g., "How DAOs are disrupting professional development")
- Create LinkedIn post generator: 150-300 word thought-leadership posts, 3x/week, with engagement hooks and relevant hashtags
- Create Twitter/X thread generator: 5-8 tweet threads breaking down complex crypto concepts for business professionals
- Add Paragraph blog post generator: weekly long-form essay synthesizing the week's narrative + training industry implications
- Set up scheduling: LinkedIn Mon/Wed/Fri 8 AM SGT, Twitter Tue/Thu 10 AM SGT, Paragraph Saturday 11 AM SGT
- Track engagement metrics and iterate on topics that get traction

### [GREEN] Corporate Training Lead Generation Engine
**Category:** Work & Career / Passive Income & Businesses
**Risk Level:** green
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
Automated lead generation system for Master's corporate training business. Monitors LinkedIn job postings, company announcements, and industry news for companies actively hiring for "digital skills," "upskilling," "workforce transformation," or "corporate training" roles. When a company is clearly investing in training (hiring L&D managers, announcing upskilling initiatives, posting RFPs), auto-generates a personalized outreach draft and queues it for Master's approval via Telegram. Also tracks existing clients for re-engagement opportunities (anniversary follow-ups, new offering announcements).

**Rationale:**
Master's primary income is corporate training, but prospecting is time-consuming and often deprioritized. Most companies signal their training needs publicly before they ever post a formal RFP — hiring an L&D manager = they need trainers; announcing a "digital transformation" = they need digital skills workshops. By systematically harvesting these signals and drafting outreach, this engine ensures a steady pipeline of warm leads without Master spending hours on LinkedIn sales navigator. Even 2-3 new clients/year from automated prospecting could represent $20-50K in additional revenue.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Build signal scanner: monitor LinkedIn (via web search patterns) for companies hiring L&D/Training roles, announcing upskilling initiatives, or posting training RFPs
- Create qualification engine: score leads by company size, industry fit, training type match, and geographic relevance (SG/APAC focus)
- Build outreach generator: draft personalized LinkedIn connection messages and emails referencing the specific signal (e.g., "Saw you're building out your L&D team — I help companies like X with digital skills workshops")
- Add re-engagement module: track past clients, auto-generate quarterly check-in drafts
- Telegram approval flow: send 3-5 leads per week with company context, signal source, and draft message — Master taps approve/edit/dismiss
- Track conversion metrics: outreach → response → meeting → closed deal
