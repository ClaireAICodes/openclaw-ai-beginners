### [GREEN] Japanese SRS Vocabulary Trainer for OpenClaw
**Category:** Learning & Skills
**Risk Level:** green
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
A lightweight, text-based spaced repetition system (SRS) delivered via OpenClaw messaging. Master gets 2-3 vocabulary flashcards each morning during his commute or free time — words auto-picked from his Duolingo progression plus JLPT N5/N1 curated lists. The system tracks which words he knows well (correct answers get pushed further out) and which need reinforcement (wrong answers come back sooner). Uses SuperMemo-2-inspired algorithm, no database needed — just a JSON file in the workspace. Delivered as interactive Telegram messages with tap-to-reveal cards.

**Rationale:**
Master uses Duolingo for Japanese learning (noted in USER.md), but Duolingo's spaced repetition can be slow and game-like. A complementary SRS system running through OpenClaw gives him targeted, efficient review of vocabulary he's learning, reinforced at optimal intervals. Low effort (just tap to answer while having coffee), high impact (accelerates retention). Pure internal tool — green risk.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Create vocabulary JSON seed files (JLPT N5-N3 word lists, 500+ entries with romaji, hiragana/katakana, English meaning, example sentence)
- Build SRS engine script (`scripts/srs-japanese.js`) implementing simplified SM-2 algorithm (ease factor, interval, repetitions tracking)
- Persist learning state to `data/japanese-srs.json` (word states: ease, interval, next review date)
- Create cron job: daily at 8:00 AM SGT → send 3 flashcards via Telegram, log responses
- Handle responses: Master taps/answers, script updates SRS state for each word
- Optional: Add weekly progress report (words mastered, weak areas, streak counter)

### [GREEN] Web3 & Crypto Weekly Alpha Research Digest
**Category:** Research & Intelligence
**Risk Level:** green
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
A curated weekly research digest that scans the entire Web3/crypto ecosystem and delivers a concise, opinionated briefing every Sunday morning. Covers: (1) **Narrative shifts** — what's trending on social/on-chain (DeFi season, RWA tokenization, ZK rollups, etc.), (2) **Notable launches** — new protocols, airdrops, testnets worth paying attention to, (3) **Market alpha** — unusual on-chain activity, whale movements, smart money tracking, (4) **Tool picks** — new developer tools, analytics platforms, or infrastructure worth knowing about. Delivered as a beautifully formatted message with links, charts, and actionable takeaways.

**Rationale:**
Master's dream projects include an NFT collection and automated trading — both require deep market awareness. He's noted as an information consumer who wants "inspiration, unique edges, and insights." Manually researching all of this takes hours. An automated digest that synthesizes the most important developments across the space, filtered through a lens of what matters for builders and traders, delivers that edge weekly. Sunday morning timing means Master starts the week informed and ready to act.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Research and identify the best free data sources: CoinGecko API, Dune Analytics public dashboards, DefiLlama, CryptoPanic RSS, GitHub trending (Web3 repos)
- Build aggregation script (`scripts/alpha-digest.js`) that fetches, scores, and summarizes across all sources
- Implement scoring/relevance filter: prioritize items related to Master's interests (NFTs, DeFi, L2s, trading tools) over noise
- Format digest with sections: Top 5 Stories, Notable Launches, Market Signals, Tools of Interest, and "What This Means for You" (actionable takeaways)
- Add cron job: Sunday 8:00 AM SGT → generate and send digest via Telegram
- Include source links and data timestamps so Master can deep-dive anything interesting
