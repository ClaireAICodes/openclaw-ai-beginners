### [GREEN] Health & Wellness Integration: Wearable Data Analyzer
**Category:** Lifestyle & Habits / Hobbies & Interests
**Risk Level:** green (data analysis only, no medical advice)
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
A comprehensive health monitoring system that aggregates data from wearables (Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin), tracks fitness metrics (steps, heart rate variability, sleep, workouts), and provides actionable insights. Includes habit formation support, goal tracking, correlation analysis (e.g., "sleep quality impacts gym performance"), and automated weekly health reports. Connects with the Fitness Tracker to optimize workout recovery and timing.

**Rationale:**
While gym is a weekly hobby, overall health and recovery are critical for performance and long-term wellbeing. Wearables generate abundant data but it's rarely synthesized into actionable insights. This system would help Master understand his health patterns, optimize recovery, and maintain consistency—turning raw metrics into a strategic advantage for his fitness goals and overall energy.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Research and catalog wearable APIs (Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin, Oura)
- Build data aggregation layer that normalizes metrics across devices
- Implement trend analysis for sleep, activity, HRV, resting heart rate
- Create correlation engine (e.g., identify how late nights affect next-day workout performance)
- Design insightful weekly report with plain-language summaries and recommendations
- Add goal tracking and streak maintenance for habit building
- Integrate with Fitness Tracker to adjust workout intensity based on recovery metrics
- Build simple dashboard to explore data and customize alert thresholds
