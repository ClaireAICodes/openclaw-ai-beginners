### [GREEN] AI-Powered Meeting & Training Session Assistant
**Category:** Apps & Code / OpenClaw Workflow and Automation
**Risk Level:** green
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
A system that automates corporate training session preparation and execution support. It ingests client requirements, researches latest industry content, generates structured slide decks and handouts, creates interactive exercises, and delivers real-time session assistance (timing, Q&A support, follow-up email drafts). Integrates with Microsoft 365 (PowerPoint, Teams) and Master's existing training workflows.

**Rationale:**
Master is a corporate trainer whose time is limited. Manual prep for each class is time-consuming, especially when clients send HTML emails with schedules. This tool transforms hours of prep into minutes, ensures content is current and relevant, and handles administrative follow-ups automatically. It directly addresses his calendar pain point and scales his training business.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Build client intake parser: extract class details (topic, duration, audience level, dates) from HTML emails; structure into a training brief
- Research engine: automatically fetch latest articles, case studies, and examples for the training topic from trusted sources; summarize and curate
- Content generator: produce slide deck outline with speaker notes, interactive exercise ideas, quiz questions, and handout materials in Master's voice
- Microsoft 365 integration: export slides to PowerPoint, create Teams meeting links, upload materials to SharePoint; use Office.js for in-app enhancements if needed
- Real-time assistant: during sessions, provide timer alerts, suggest answers to participant questions (drawn from research), capture Q&A topics for follow-up
- Follow-up automation: generate personalized thank-you emails, share materials, and request feedback; log to CRM or spreadsheet
- Template library: maintain reusable templates for common training topics to speed up future prep

### [GREEN] Smart Fitness & Nutrition Planning System
**Category:** Lifestyle & Habits / Research & Intelligence
**Risk Level:** green
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
A personalized fitness and nutrition system that creates adaptive workout plans and meal prep guides based on Master's goals (e.g., muscle gain, fat loss), schedule, dietary preferences, and wearable data (if available). It tracks progress, adjusts plans dynamically, generates grocery lists, and provides recipe recommendations with cooking instructions. Includes integration with popular fitness apps (Apple Health, Google Fit, Strava) and recipe APIs.

**Rationale:**
Master goes to the gym weekly but likely does manual planning for workouts and meals. As a busy professional, optimizing health is important for sustained energy and appearance. This tool automates the planning cycle, ensures nutritional targets are met, and removes decision fatigue around food and exercise. It aligns with his efficiency mindset and desire for automated self-improvement.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Goal configuration wizard: set objectives (strength, hypertrophy, endurance), available days, time per session, equipment access (home vs gym), dietary restrictions
- Workout generator: produce structured routines with exercises, sets, reps, rest periods; include proper form cues and progressive overload tracking; export to Apple Watch or gym app
- Nutrition planner: calculate caloric and macro targets; generate weekly meal plans with recipes; adjust based on daily adherence and progress
- Grocery list builder: aggregate ingredients from meal plans, optimize for shopping efficiency, export to Notes or Reminders
- Wearable integration: sync with Apple Health/Google Fit to import workout data, rest days, and biometrics (heart rate, sleep); use this to auto-adcompute recovery and intensity
- Progress dashboard: track weight, strength metrics, body measurements, and photos; visualize trends and suggest plan modifications
- Recipe curator: fetch recipes from APIs (Spoonacular, Edamam) matching macro targets and preferences; include cooking timers and step-by-step instructions
