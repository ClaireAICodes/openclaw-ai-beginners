### [GREEN] Passive Income Idea Engine & Feasibility Scorer
**Category:** Passive Income & Businesses
**Risk Level:** green
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
A research pipeline that continuously scans for passive income opportunities across models: digital product licensing (fonts, icons, templates), print-on-demand niches, affiliate content arbitrage, and Crown/Ember style micro-SaaS. Each opportunity is scored on effort-to-automation ratio, competition, and monetization potential, then produces a ranked weekly digest with a "start score" (0–100) indicating how close it is to being fully automatable. Includes links to suppliers, keywords, and a rough implementation plan.

**Rationale:**
Master wants a self-running business but the search space is vast. This engine narrows the field to only those ideas that are realistically automatable (e.g., drop-shipped design goods, stock media stores, niche affiliate sites with auto-generated content) and provides actionable next steps. It turns passive income ideation into a systematic, data-driven process.

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Define data sources: Etsy/Redbubble/Amazon Merch trends, Google Trends, affiliate network directories (CJ, ShareASale), GitHub trending templates
- Build scrapers to collect opportunity signals (search volume, competition, product count)
- Train a scoring model (heuristic-based) on factors: automation potential (can it be generated?), maintenance burden, commission rates, SEO difficulty
- Generate weekly digest: top 5 opportunities, feasibility score, estimated setup time, and suggested tech (Shopify, Gumroad, WordPress + auto-blogging)
- Store historical scores to track trend momentum
- Add feedback: Master can mark ideas "pursued" or "junk" to refine scoring

### [GREEN] OpenClaw Skill Marketplace & Auto-Monetization Layer
**Category:** Passive Income & Businesses
**Risk Level:** green
**Confidence:** High

**Description:**
Convert the OpenClaw skills ecosystem into a revenue stream by packaging skills as installable products with tiered pricing (free/one-time/subscription). Build a lightweight marketplace where users can browse, purchase, and install skills directly. Includes license management, usage analytics, and Stripe integration. Master's existing skills become initial catalog; revenue split incentivizes external contributors.

**Rationale:**
Master is deeply invested in OpenClaw; turning skill development into a business leverages his expertise and existing codebase. A marketplace provides clear monetization path with recurring revenue potential. Starting with his own skills reduces risk and provides immediate catalog. GREEN because it's internal tooling to enable future monetization; actual payment processing would be YELLOW (requires approval to connect Stripe).

**Implementation Tasks:**
- Design skill package format: zip with manifest (name, version, description, dependencies, permissions)
- Build marketplace frontend (simple Next.js) listing skills with screenshots, docs, pricing
- Implement license server: generates cryptographically signed license keys per purchase
- Modify OpenClaw skill installer to accept license verification before enabling tools
- Integrate Stripe Checkout for payments; webhook to grant access
- Add usage metering: track installations, active days, feature usage for tier enforcement
- Create contributor portal: allow others to submit skills (review before listing)
- Draft terms and privacy policy
- Deploy on Vercel; use PostgreSQL/Supabase for data
