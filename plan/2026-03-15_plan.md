**Resource Requirements:**

**Feasibility Assessment:**

- ⚠️ Master must provide Gmail OAuth credentials and authorize the app

**Implementation Steps:**

2. **Development Phase** (6h): Build rule-based + LLM classifier (5 categories: urgent/newsletter/notification/spam/personal), auto-actions (label, archive, flag), morning digest generator (top 5 urgent via Telegram), feedback loop via Telegram thumbs up/down

**Success Criteria:**

- Classification accuracy >85% after 2 weeks of feedback
